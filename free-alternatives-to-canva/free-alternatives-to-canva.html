Frequently asked questions

What is the best free alternative to Canva?

It depends on the job. For templates and design, Figma (Free Starter plan) and Adobe Express (free plan by Adobe) are the closest matches. For photo editing, Photopea is a free Photoshop-style editor in your browser. For a website, Framer lets you publish 1 website free.

Are these Canva alternatives really free?

Yes — each one has a real free plan on the brand’s own official site, and none of them asks for a card. Paid upgrades exist for extra features, but the free plans above cover everyday design, photo editing and a simple website.

Can I use these instead of Canva Pro?

For most everyday jobs, yes: posters, social posts, photo edits and a simple website are all covered by the free plans here. Canva Pro adds premium templates and extras, so if a design needs a Pro-only template or element, that part stays paid.
