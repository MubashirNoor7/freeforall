How to get free Disney Plus trial without credit card

How to get free Disney Plus trial without credit card? The honest answer first: you cannot, because there is no Disney+ trial at all. Disney+ offered a 7-day free trial when it launched in 2019, then ended it in 2020, and it has not brought one back since. So there is no trial to join with a card or without one. The real ways to watch Disney+ free are through a partner: Verizon in the US, O2 in the UK, and Tesco Clubcard vouchers in the UK. Watch out for sites selling “free Disney+ accounts” or trial links — those are not real routes. The real ways to watch Disney+ free

1. Verizon, in the US — 6 months free on selected plans

Verizon Unlimited plans have included 6 months of Disney+ Premium free. The exact perk changes over time, so check Verizon’s own page for the plan you are on before you switch, and cancel before the free months end or it turns into a paid plan. See our guide on how to cancel a free trial without getting charged for the safe way to do it.

2. O2, in the UK — up to 6 months free

New O2 customers, and customers who upgrade to a pay-monthly plan, can get up to 6 months of Disney+ free as an Extra on their mobile plan. If you are already an O2 customer and do not want to upgrade, you can add Disney+ to your plan and get £2 a month off instead.

3. Tesco Clubcard, in the UK — 3 months from vouchers

Tesco Clubcard members can swap their vouchers at double value for a 3-month Disney+ subscription. This is not free in the strict sense — you pay with vouchers you already earned from shopping — but it costs no new money. The Premium plan is not included in the Clubcard offer.

Why Disney+ ended its trial

Disney+ gave new subscribers a 7-day free trial when it launched in late 2019. It ended the trial in the US in 2020, and the last trials elsewhere ended by late 2021. Disney’s own help centre confirms that no free trials are offered to new or returning subscribers today, and Disney has not announced plans to bring one back.
