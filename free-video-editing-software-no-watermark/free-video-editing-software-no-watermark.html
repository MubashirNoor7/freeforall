What “no watermark” means

A watermark is the app’s logo stamped across your exported video. Some free editors add one to every export. CapCut’s free plan does not — a video you cut yourself from your own clips in the standard free editor exports clean. Two things can still add a mark: a Pro-marked template, effect or stock asset, and a template’s ending clip. Delete those from your timeline before you export, and check the export screen on capcut.com — that screen is the final word.

Frequently asked questions

Does CapCut free really export without a watermark?

Yes, for a video you edit yourself. CapCut’s own help pages say a project built from your own clips in the standard free editor exports without a watermark. A watermark appears only when the project uses a Pro-marked template, effect or stock asset, or a template’s ending clip — remove those before export and the video comes out clean.

How do I avoid the CapCut watermark?

Start a new project from your own clips instead of a template, use only free assets (anything marked Pro can lock or watermark the export), delete the template ending clip from the end of the timeline, and export at 1080p. Then check the export screen before you publish — if it shows a watermark warning, a Pro item is still on the timeline.

Is CapCut free on phone and PC?

Yes. CapCut is a free video editor for phone and PC, and there is a web editor too. The free plan covers everyday editing; 4K and 60 fps export and premium templates are paid Pro features.

Can I edit videos in Canva for free?

Yes. Canva’s free plan includes a simple video editor for quick edits — trimming clips, adding text and making social posts. For longer or more detailed edits, CapCut is the better free pick on this page.
