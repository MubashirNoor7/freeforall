The real ways to get LinkedIn Premium free

1. The Premium free trial

LinkedIn offers a free trial of Premium, usually for one month — LinkedIn says the length can vary by region or promotion. A valid credit card is needed to start it, and the trial turns into a paid plan unless you cancel before it ends. Start it on linkedin.com/premium, and read our guide on how to cancel a free trial without getting charged before you sign up.

2. Twelve months free for the US military community

LinkedIn’s Social Impact program works with Hire Heroes USA, Student Veterans of America and Hiring Our Heroes. Eligible US veterans, military spouses and transitioning Active Duty service members can get a one-time 12-month Premium subscription through career services with one of those partners. After the year it ends on its own. Details are on LinkedIn’s military and veterans page.

3. No student discount, no free-forever Premium

LinkedIn lists no student price for Premium, and there is no official way to keep Premium free forever. The free trial is the route LinkedIn itself offers everyone. The basic LinkedIn account — profiles, job search and applying — stays free.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get LinkedIn Premium free trial?

Yes. LinkedIn offers a free trial of Premium, usually for one month — LinkedIn says the length can vary by region or promotion. You need a valid credit card to start, and it becomes a paid plan unless you cancel before the trial ends.

Does the LinkedIn Premium free trial need a card?

Yes. LinkedIn says you must enter a valid credit card to start the trial. You are not charged during the trial, but the subscription converts to a paid plan automatically at the end unless you cancel first.

Can I get another free trial after cancelling one?

Not for at least 12 months. LinkedIn says that if you cancel a free Premium trial, you won't be eligible to sign up for another free trial for at least 12 months — and if you've already used a trial, you may not be eligible for another at all.

Is there a free LinkedIn Premium for students or the military?

For the military community, yes: eligible US veterans, military spouses and transitioning Active Duty service members can get a one-time 12-month Premium subscription through LinkedIn's nonprofit partners. For students, no: LinkedIn lists no student price for Premium — the free trial is the route it offers everyone.
