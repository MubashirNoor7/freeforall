Frequently asked questions

Is there a truly free logo maker with no sign up at all?

Most good logo tools ask for a free email account so you can save your work and download the file — that is what “no sign up” means on this page: no card and no paid plan, not no account at all. Canva, Figma and Adobe Express all start free with an email account, and none of them asks for a card.

Which free logo maker is best for beginners?

Canva is the easiest place to start: its free plan includes 250,000+ templates, so you pick a logo template, change the name and colours, and download it. Adobe Express is just as simple for quick social and flyer designs, while Figma gives you more control if you like to design from scratch.

Can I download my logo for free?

Yes, on the free plans — but check first. Use only templates, fonts and graphics marked free: premium elements are paid extras, and downloading a design that uses them can ask you to upgrade. A plain PNG download of a free design costs nothing.

Do I need a card to use these logo makers?

No. All three free plans start without a card, and the paid upgrades are optional. If any page asks for card details for a “free” logo, stop — that is not one of the free plans listed here.
