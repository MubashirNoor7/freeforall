Frequently asked questions

Is there really no sign-up at all?

Not for the well-known tools. Leonardo AI, ChatGPT and Google Gemini all ask you to create a free account with an email address before you can generate images. Any site that promises a big-name AI image generator with no account at all should be treated with care — check it is the real official site before you use it.

Do any of these ask for a credit card?

No. None of the three tools on this page asks for a card on its free plan. You sign up with an email address, and the free image generation starts from there. Paid plans exist, but they are optional.

Which free AI image generator is best?

Leonardo AI is the best pick if you mainly want pictures — it is made for images, game art and posters, with free credits every day. ChatGPT and Google Gemini are better if you want images as part of a chat, for example asking for a picture while you are writing or studying.

Can I use the images I generate for my business or social posts?

That depends on each tool’s terms, which can change. Check the terms on the tool’s official site before using a generated image for business, selling, or ads — especially for logos or anything you plan to earn money from.
